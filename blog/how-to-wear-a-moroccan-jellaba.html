How to Wear a Moroccan Jellaba with Confidence: A Style Guide

By Khadija · October 1, 2026

A jellaba is comfortable, elegant and surprisingly easy to style. If you live in the United States or Europe, here is how to wear one so it looks natural in your daily life.

Start with the right size

A jellaba is meant to be loose, but it should still sit well on the shoulders. Check that the sleeves reach your wrist or just below, and that the hem falls between mid-calf and ankle. If it is too long, you can ask a tailor to adjust the hem without touching the trim.

Layer it like a coat

Think of the jellaba as an outer layer. Wear it over a plain top and straight trousers, or over a simple dress. In colder weather, add a thin knit underneath. Keep your layers slim so the garment keeps its clean line.

Choose colours that work for you

  • Neutrals such as beige, camel and cream go with everything and show Randa detail beautifully.
  • Deep colours like burgundy, emerald and navy look rich and formal.
  • Gold and mustard make a statement for celebrations.

Shoes and accessories

Flat leather sandals, ankle boots or simple sneakers all work. Keep jewellery light so the embroidery stays the star. A small bag and a soft scarf are enough.

Day versus occasion

For everyday wear, choose a simple cut with subtle trim. For a dinner or a celebration, pick a richer fabric, a statement colour and more detailed Randa. The same garment can be dressed up with heels and a clutch.

Wearing it with respect and joy

The jellaba is part of Moroccan heritage. Wearing it with care for the craft, and understanding how the trim is made, adds meaning. If you want to understand that craft, start with our Randa explainer or explore the collection.
