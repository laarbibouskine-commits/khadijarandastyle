Moroccan Randa Embroidery for Beginners: Tools and Materials You Need

By Khadija · October 1, 2026

One of the best things about Randa is how little you need to get started. You do not need a sewing machine or a workshop. A small kit and a quiet corner are enough. Here is what to buy and what to skip.

1. Needles

Choose a fine, sharp needle with an eye large enough for your thread. A small pack of mixed sizes is a smart first purchase, because the right size depends on your fabric and thread. If the needle leaves holes you can see, it is too thick.

2. Thread

Pick a smooth, strong thread that does not fray easily. For your first samples, choose a colour that contrasts clearly with your fabric so you can see every stitch and correct mistakes. Buy two or three colours rather than a huge set.

3. Practice fabric

Use a medium-weight, plain-weave cotton or similar cloth. Avoid very stretchy or very slippery fabrics at the start. Cut several squares of about 20 cm so you can practise a different pattern on each one.

4. Small tools

  • Sharp small scissors for clean thread ends.
  • Fabric marker or chalk to draw guide lines you can wipe away.
  • Ruler to keep your lines straight.
  • Thimble (optional) if you stitch for long sessions.

5. Good light

Fine stitching is easier and kinder to your eyes in daylight or under a bright lamp. This is the cheapest upgrade you can make.

What you can skip for now

Skip expensive specialty threads, large frames and complicated organisers. Once you know which stitches you enjoy, you can invest in better materials with confidence.

Next step

With your kit ready, begin with a short daily practice. The playbook gives you a 30-day plan with one small exercise per day, so you build skill without feeling overwhelmed. You can find our favourite tools in the shop.
