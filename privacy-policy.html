Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Khadija Randa Style ("we", "us") handles your personal data when you visit our website, join our email list or buy our products. We aim to collect as little data as possible.

1. Who is responsible

Khadija Randa Style is the data controller. You can reach us at hello@khadijarandastyle.com.

2. What we collect

  • Email address if you sign up for our list or request a free pattern.
  • Order information (name, email, billing country, products bought) when you purchase. Card details are processed by our payment provider and never stored by us.
  • Messages you send us by email, including questions about made-to-order pieces.
  • Technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, collected by our hosting provider and by analytics tools if we enable them.
  • Language preference saved in your browser (local storage) so the site shows in the language you chose. It is not sent to us.

3. How we use your data

  • To deliver the products you buy and provide support.
  • To send you the free pattern and, if you agreed, news and offers. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To keep the website secure and working, and to understand how it is used.
  • To meet legal and tax obligations.

4. Legal bases (EU and UK visitors)

We rely on your consent for marketing emails, contract for delivering what you buy, legitimate interests for security and basic site analytics, and legal obligation for accounting records.

5. Who we share data with

We share data only with service providers who help us run the business, under appropriate agreements. These may include the website host (Vercel), payment and checkout providers, an email service provider, and analytics tools. We do not sell your personal data.

6. Affiliate links

Some links in our shop and blog lead to retailers such as Amazon or Temu and may contain a tracking code that tells the retailer you came from our site, so that we can earn a commission. When you click such a link you leave our site, and the retailer's own privacy policy and cookies apply. We do not receive your purchase details or personal data from these retailers, only aggregated commission reports.

7. Third-party content

Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests them, Google may receive your IP address. Payment providers may set their own cookies on their checkout pages.

8. Cookies

This site does not use advertising cookies. If we add analytics or marketing tools that need cookies, we will ask for your consent where the law requires it and update this policy.

9. International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries outside your own, including the United States. Where required, we use safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

10. How long we keep data

We keep email list data until you unsubscribe, order records for as long as the law requires for accounting, and support emails for as long as needed to help you.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or California law), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to your local data protection authority. California residents may also ask us not to sell or share their data, and we do not sell it. To use your rights, email hello@khadijarandastyle.com.

12. Children

Our website is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, but no online service can be completely secure.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

15. Contact

Questions? Email hello@khadijarandastyle.com.
