What is Randa?

Randa is the delicate hand-stitched trim that runs down the front, hood and sleeves of a Moroccan jellaba. Every curve, zigzag and knot is made by hand, which is why no two pieces are ever the same.

For generations it was passed from mother to daughter. Now Khadija shares it with women everywhere, with a method that is easy to follow, even if you have never threaded a needle.

  • Understand the stitches and the tools
  • Practise on simple patterns first
  • Finish a real piece you can wear or gift
